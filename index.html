Roomba Controller Simulation

PYTHON · 12 FUNCTIONS · RECURSION · RANDOMISED OBSTACLES

A simulated robot vacuum that has to clean a room it can’t fully see. It advances in 5-metre steps, works against a 600-second (10-minute) limit, and every move carries a 20% chance of hitting an obstacle — forcing it to back off, re-plan, and resume a recursive sweep until the room is covered or time runs out.

Blackjack — Multi-Player Card Engine

PYTHON · FISHER–YATES SHUFFLE · 1–6 PLAYERS · DEALER STANDS ON 17

A full Blackjack engine for 1–6 players against the dealer. The deck is shuffled with a proper Fisher–Yates shuffle (not random swaps), the dealer stands on 17, and — faithfully to my assignment code — face cards keep their rank values: J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13, which makes busts come fast. It even includes cheat detection: try to quietly edit your hand to 21 and the engine catches it.
